The things you do every day.

Clipboard

That thing you copied? Still here.

Text and images are saved as you copy. Search them, preview images, Option-click to paste straight in, or pin an image to the desktop. You decide how long to keep things and which apps to skip — or switch it off anytime.

Windows

Windows, right where you want them.

Halves, quarters, thirds, a 3×3 grid, centered, moved to another display or put back — 29 layouts, each on a shortcut.

App shortcuts

One keystroke. Right app.

Give your favorite apps a shortcut. Nivlet opens them or brings them forward, and lets you know if two shortcuts clash.

Browser routing

Every link, in the right browser.

Chat links in Safari, work sites in Chrome. Set rules by source app or website and everything else goes to your fallback — including links from the few apps that don’t say where they came from.

System & input

Know what your Mac is up to.

Network speed sits in the menu bar; CPU, memory, disk and IP addresses are a click away. Pick an input source for each app and it switches for you.

Screenshot

Capture it. Mark it up. Read it.

Press a shortcut and the screen freezes so you can select an area or a window. Mark it up with boxes, arrows, pen, text and mosaic, then copy, save or pin it to the desktop. Text and QR codes in the shot are recognized right on your Mac.

Translation

Double-tap. Understood.

Select text and double-tap a modifier key, or pick an area of the screen. macOS’s built-in translation does the rest, on device. It picks the direction for you and remembers your last choice. Requires macOS 15 or later.

Your data stays
on your Mac.

No sign-up. Your settings, clipboard, screenshots, text recognition and translations never leave your Mac, and the core tools work offline.

Gone when you quit

Clipboard history is cleared when you quit, by default. If you choose to keep it, it’s stored as plain text on your Mac — so pause recording before copying anything sensitive.

Signed, notarized, your call

Every release is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Updates install only when you say so.

No tracking

This site has no analytics or tracking cookies. The app goes online only to check for updates or open a link.

Up and running in three steps.

  1. 1

    Download

    Grab the DMG or ZIP, drag Nivlet into Applications and open it.

  2. 2

    Allow access

    Arranging windows, Option-paste and selection translation need Accessibility access; screenshots need Screen Recording. Just follow the prompts — there’s no need to turn off Gatekeeper.

  3. 3

    Make it yours

    Click the N in the menu bar, open Settings, turn on what you want and click Save All Settings. For browser routing, set Nivlet as your default browser.
