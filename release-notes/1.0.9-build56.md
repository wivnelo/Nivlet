Nivlet 1.0.9: A unified sidebar for eight settings pages; local HTML/HTM forwarding; Save All Settings with stable footer feedback; Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese and native menu resources. Clipboard recording uses one enable switch in its own card instead of pause/resume, with existing history and retention preserved. Click a thumbnail, or focus it and press Enter or Space, to open an image preview without a separate preview button. Previews close outside the image or with Escape; focus restoration after asynchronous history refresh is fixed. Includes targeted stability fixes and removal of development-only framework files. Apple Silicon (arm64), macOS 13+ only. Version 1.0.8 Build 55 is the final Intel release; historical downloads remain available.
